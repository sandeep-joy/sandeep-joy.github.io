I am an inaugural Bhaumik Postdoctoral Fellow at [University of California Los Angeles](https://www.pa.ucla.edu). Previously, I was a Quantum Initiative Postdoctoral Fellow at the [National High Magnetic Field Laboratory](https://nationalmaglab.org) and [Florida State University](https://physics.fsu.edu). I am a theoretical condensed matter physicist interested in understanding quantum materials.

I received my Ph.D. from The Ohio State University, where I worked with Prof. [Brian Skinner](https://sites.google.com/view/skinner-physics). At the MagLab and FSU, I was supervised by Prof. [Cyprian Lewandowski](https://physics.fsu.edu/person/cyprian-lewandowski). At UCLA, my formal supervisor is Prof. [Anshul Kogar](https://kogargroup.physics.ucla.edu) (yes, an experimentalist, lol). I actively collaborate with theorists and experimentalists at UCLA and beyond.
