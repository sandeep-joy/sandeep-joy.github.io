I am an Inaugural Bhaumik Postdoctoral Fellow at UCLA. Prior to that, I was a Quantum Initiative postdoctoral fellow at the [National High Magnetic Field Laboratory](https://nationalmaglab.org) and [Florida State University](https://physics.fsu.edu). My research focuses on theoretical condensed matter physics, with a primary emphasis on exploring quantum materials. 

I completed my Ph.D. at The Ohio State University, where I worked with Prof. [Brian Skinner](https://sites.google.com/view/skinner-physics) in the Physics Department. At MagLab and FSU, I was formally supervised by Prof. [Cyprian Lewandowski](https://physics.fsu.edu/person/cyprian-lewandowski). At UCLA Prof. [Anshul Kogar] is formal supervisor. I actively collaborate with theorists and experimentalists both at my home institution and beyond.

You can reach me at sjoy AT physics.ucla DOT edu.
